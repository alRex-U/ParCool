# Hide In Block

Next you will see the worst parkour-like in this mod, but pretty useful action. It is named *Hide In Block*.
You can crawl and hide into block clusters of some kind of blocks, such as leaf blocks, and hay blocks.

While you hide in blocks, enemies become hard to find you.

Additionally, you can start hiding by diving into blocks when you land.

## How to use

1. Point your cursor to blocks you want to hide in
2. Press <key name="key.sneak"/> and <key name="key.parcool.hide_in_block"/>
