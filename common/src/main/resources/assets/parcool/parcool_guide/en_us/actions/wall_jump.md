# Wall Jump

You can jump in air by kicking wall.
This should be useful for many situations like getting over low obstacles, or gain height by kicking continuously.

It's also possible to use with other actions like [Horizontal Fast Run](parcool:actions/horizontal_fast_run.md)
or [Hang On](parcool:actions/hang_on.md)

As a point to note, you must have wall in your back to trigger *Wall Jump*.

## How to use

1. Press <key name="key.jump"/> key in air nearby wall
