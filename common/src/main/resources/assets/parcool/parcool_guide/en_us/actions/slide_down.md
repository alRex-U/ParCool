# Slide Down

ParCool provides some actions to reduce landing damage like [Breakfall](parcool:actions/breakfall.md).
*Slide Down* is also one of such actions. You decelerate by using friction of wall.

Be careful that you take little damage when use this action without <item id="parcool:traceur_glove"/>.
Please see [equipments](parcool:equipments.md) about how to make <item id="parcool:traceur_glove"/>.

# How to use

1. Press <key name="key.parcool.slide_down"/> nearby wall when you are falling
