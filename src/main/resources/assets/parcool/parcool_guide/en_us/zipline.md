# Zipline

ParCool provides facility named **Zipline**.
Zipline is a rope bound to two **zipline hooks**. You can use it to move along the rope.

## Place Zipline

First you have to place *zipline hooks* where you want to.

<recipe id="parcool:wooden_zipline_hook"/>
<recipe id="parcool:iron_zipline_hook"/>

Then next you have to craft <item id="parcool:zipline_rope"/>.

<recipe id="parcool:zipline_rope"/>

Zipline rope is *dyeable*, so it's possible to color rope by crafting it with dye items.  
Shift + Click the rope to change tightness.

After that, use your rope to hooks to place zipline.

## How to use

See [Ride Zipline](parcool:actions/ride_zipline.md)

## Powered Zipline

When you input redstone signal to your hook, the zipline will be *powered zipline*.  
Powered zipline give acceleration bigger than gravity, so it enable you to go upward by zipline.

As a point to note, you take damage if you use *powered zipline* without <item id="parcool:traceur_gloves"/>.  
Please see [equipments](parcool:equipments.md) about how to make <item id="parcool:traceur_gloves"/>.
