# Trick Jump

Acrobat performance is one of famous aspects in parkour. Yes, now you can do it! Let's try.

*Trick Jump* has some variant, such as front-flip, back-flip and stride-jump, etc.
Which variant is triggered is decided by the situation which you are in.

## How to use

1. Press movement keys and jump simultaneously

or

1. Jump while doing [Fast Run](parcool:actions/fast_run.md)
