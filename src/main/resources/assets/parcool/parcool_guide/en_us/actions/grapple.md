# Use Grapple

*Grappling Hook* is equipment to provide additional spatial movement.

See [Grapple](parcool:grapple.md) for the details.

You can equip <item id="parcool:grappling_hook"/> by holding it in your hand.

While you are equipping it, you should see the grapple target at the center of screen.
You can shoot a hook by pressing <key name="key.attack"/> toward its target,
and you can wind up the wire by pressing <key name="key.use"/> after the hook attached to block.

You cannot use <item id="parcool:grappling_hook"/> to move upward,
the winding up power is not enough to pull up defying gravity.

But still it should be useful for horizontal movement, and swinging you by attaching it to high place.
