# Dive

Dive is triggered when you jump off from high ledge.
It includes special animation.

In addition, Dive can derive into [Skydive](parcool:actions/skydive.md)
and [Hide In Block - Dive variant](parcool:actions/hide_in_block.md). See it also.

## How to use

1. Do [Fast Run](parcool:actions/fast_run.md)
2. Jump off from high ledge
