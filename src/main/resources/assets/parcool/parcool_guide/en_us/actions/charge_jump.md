# Charge Jump

You can use *Charge Jump* to jump higher than usual. It enables you to reach almost twice height of normal jump.

By using with [Hang On](parcool:actions/hang_on.md) and [Climb Up](parcool:actions/climb_up.md), you can get over 4
block height obstacles at maximum.

## How to use

1. Continue to press <key name="key.sneak"/> key to charge power
2. Jump
