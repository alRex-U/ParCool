# Grapple

*Grappling Hook* is equipment to provide additional spatial movement.
It shoots a hook with wire, and pull you up by winding up the wire.

The grapple in this mod, is designed not to be used for vertical movement.
It's mainly used for horizontal quick move and swinging.

First you have to craft it.
